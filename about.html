My setup

The desk

The monitor is on an arm, so the only things standing on the desk are the ones I touch: keyboard, headphones, the microphone on its own boom, the interface behind it and the drawing tablet. The tower is on the floor underneath. What is lit is the wall, not the desk.

The keyboard

All black: black case, black keycaps, nothing printed on them that catches the light. It sits square in front of the monitor with the cable running back over the edge of the desk.

The mouse

Wireless, symmetric and matte black, on the cloth pad that takes up the right half of the desk. There is no cable in any frame of the clip because there is no cable.

The Pi

The homelab end of all this: a Raspberry Pi 5 with an NVMe HAT on the GPIO header and a Samsung 970 EVO Plus in the slot, so it runs off an SSD rather than an SD card. The Minecraft server behind Stalkr runs on a Pi 5 like this one.

Building things

When I was young, I played with Lego a lot. It stimulated a part of my brain that likes to create stuff out of blocks. I still like doing it.

Sports

I have always liked to do sports. It really makes me feel free and light, especially running as fast as I can. I believe that doing sports has developed my competitive side.

Flow

Sometimes, when I really like to do something, I am able to fully concentrate on the task. Listening to music makes me happy.

Anime

I like to watch anime, because they don't mirror real life. They are like alternate universes. I could see myself living in some of them.

Games

Playing games is a way of expressing myself online, spending time creating characters or profiles. I do play a lot of different genres though. Some of them are competitive, some are technical and some are just pure fun. I play games either with friends, or by myself.

In games, where you have to shoot at a target, you need good aim. My good aim is probably why I like such competitive games that much. Being able to win against others with just raw technique makes me feel unstoppable. It is pure dopamine!

Languages

Learning languages is something I have been doing more passively in the past years. Even so, I enjoy learning them very much. At the moment I am learning Mandarin Chinese. It is a language that really fascinates me.
