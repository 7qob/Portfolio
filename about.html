About

Who I am, what I'm into, and what I'm looking for.

I'm a 17 year old computer science student in Bern, Switzerland. Most of what I build starts as something I wanted for myself — a tool, a server, a small game.

That is where everything on this site came from: a reverse proxy so my own LLM server can be reached from outside without being left open (Ignite), a chat frontend that never leaves my machine (Kobui), a dashboard for the Minecraft server on my Raspberry Pi (Stalkr), and a ComfyUI workflow I kept tuning until it was worth reusing.

Outside of code I draw, mod games, and dig deep into topics I'm curious about, cybersecurity especially.

Right now I'm looking for a Praktikum, focused on security tooling, self-hosting, and AI.
